My interest lies in how we can apply AI in a meaningful way to help navigate the climate crisis. My work involves applying interpretable machine learning techniques to develop models that aid in climate risk estimation.

My PhD focused on enhancing the resolution of tropical cyclone rainfall using generative machine learning techniques. By working with generative models, I was able to produce high-resolution rainfall estimates for 28,000 synthetic tropical cyclone events in the order of hours. I have found that machine learning can complement climate science by delivering tangible efficiency savings without sacrificing performance.
