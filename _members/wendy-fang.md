I am a current PhD student at the School of Geographical Sciences, University of Bristol. My research focuses on integrating meteorological and health data to assess the health risks in the future.

Having obtained a background in Public Administration (BA) and Public Health (MSc), my interests have evolved towards the intersection of climate change and public health, particularly the impact of extreme temperature – pollutant events on cognitive health.
