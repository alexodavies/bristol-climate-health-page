I am a PhD student in the School of Geographical Sciences and work as a Climate Scientist at the Met Office in the Climate Impacts Modelling Team. My research is focused on compound events, and I am currently looking at compound drought-heatwave events in Brazil, and how these may change in the future. I am supervised by Rachel James and Dann Mitchell.

Having previously studied for a Masters at Bristol (MSc Climate Change Science and Policy), I am excited to be continuing my studies in climate science.

I enjoy running (especially trail running), reading, and drinking coffee!
