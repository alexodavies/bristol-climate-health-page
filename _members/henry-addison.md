My research is in the field of AI for climate science. I am studying the use of machine learning to complement dynamical downscaling with cheaper methods to generate high-resolution climate projections. I am also interested in how to ensure these methods benefit producers and consumers of projection datasets.

For my PhD, I developed CPMGEM (Convection-Permitting Model Generative EMulator), an application of a generative machine learning model, a diffusion model, that skilfully emulates a convection-permitting model. This achieves similar results on England and Wales domain at daily timescales to dynamical downscaling at a fraction of the computational cost compared to the Met Office’s CPM.
