Our mission

A healthier future in a changing climate.

We bridge climate science and epidemiology to understand health risks in today’s climate, attribute impacts to climate change, explore future risks, and improve preparedness for weather extremes. Our work spans observations, large climate-model ensembles, statistical health models and partnerships with decision-makers.

02 / Why it matters

Research with
real-world reach.

Our research asks not only how climate and weather affect health, but how that evidence can improve preparedness. We study plausible extreme heat mortality, near-real-time heat-health impacts, longer-term mortality and morbidity, and the changing risks health systems need to plan for.

Our work connects climate science with epidemiology and public health, and has involved collaboration with organisations including the Met Office and UK health agencies.
