The University of Bristol has created a new Climate Change and Health research strand led jointly by the Cabot Institute for the Environment and the Elizabeth Blackwell Institute for Health Research. These four short videos, featuring Dann Mitchell and Eunice Lo along with other leading academics, briefly explain why this area of research is so important: Why look at this complex challenge? ‘How we are working together’ and ‘A Healthier Future’.
