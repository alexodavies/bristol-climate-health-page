Regan Mudhar along with with other PhD students and postdocs demonstrate the benefits of studying and working in science for people and planet. They talk about climate adaptation in the UK and abroad and show how young people can be involved in making a positive difference, including sharing the route they took from school to their current position. 

This video is a great way to enhance science, geography, climate change and careers sessions at KS3 and beyond. More science resources can be found at [Science Resources for KS3](https://www.nercgw4plus.ac.uk/science-resources-for-ks3).
