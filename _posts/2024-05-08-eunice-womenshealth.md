Heatwaves are increasing in intensity and frequency. There is a gap in research on the impacts of heat stress on women's health. The Cabot Institute Seedcorn Fund supported an interdisciplinary project to explore this topic with a focus on how pregnant women are uniquely effected. In this video, Eunice Lo explains more about the work and it's impact.
