summary: The vision of Futureproof is to enhance resilience of NHS emergency care for future changes in extreme weather and climate. We will pioneer research on forecasting daily-to-seasonal surges in emergency hospital and ambulance demand and producing decadal-to-centennial climate-health projections. We will show how these demands would shift in plausible worst-case conditions, and in future climate and socio-economic scenarios. Through co-design with policy partners, we will help translate the science into tangible interventions across the UK, setting a gold-standard for climate-resilient healthcare.
